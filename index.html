The Challenge

Every child communicates differently. For children with intellectual and developmental disabilities, expressing emotions, needs, discomfort, or frustration can sometimes be difficult — and important observations can become scattered between home, school, and other support environments.

Give every child a simpler way to communicate.

Child Mode and the Communication Board use large visual cards paired with text and Text-to-Speech, so children can express how they feel and what they need.

Understanding the User

Target demographic analysis, common technology barriers, and user persona.

Target Demographic: Children with IDD

NeuroVoice focuses specifically on children with intellectual and developmental disabilities. These children often communicate through non-verbal cues, gestures, or behavioral expressions rather than conventional speech.

Common Technology Barriers

  • Complex navigation menus and small text buttons
  • Interfaces requiring rapid multi-step instructions
  • Lack of audio/visual feedback during interactions
  • Overwhelming sensory stimuli (flashing animations, cluttered screens)

Responsible AI & Child Privacy

Ethical positioning, prototype boundaries, and data safeguards.

🛡️ Responsible AI Framework

NeuroVoice is an assistive tool to support communication and observational logging. It is NOT a clinical diagnosis system, psychiatric assessment tool, or medical device.

  • All AI suggestions and pattern summaries are observational and rule-based in this prototype.
  • Suggestions must always be reviewed by qualified parents, educators, or professionals.
  • No medical diagnostic claims are made by the platform.

🔒 Child Data & Local Privacy

In this static frontend prototype, all child profiles, logs, and preferences are stored exclusively inside your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). No personal data is transmitted to external backend servers or cloud services.
